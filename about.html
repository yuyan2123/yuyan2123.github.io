自我介紹

For me, the best part of programming is taking a vague idea and turning it, step by step, into something that actually runs, gets used, and solves a real problem.

My relationship with computers started in the Data Processing track at Tainan Commercial Senior High. Writing my first programs there, little Windows games in Visual Basic, hooked me: watching an idea in my head become something moving on screen was the reason I couldn’t stop. Around the same time, word processing earned me a Gold Hand Award at the National High School Commercial Skills Competition.

Studying Information Management at National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology, I started pushing that curiosity toward real problems. The moment I remember most was my first year: I spent a single day writing a course-registration bot and packed my whole schedule with it, the first time I felt that code could directly change my own life. An IM background also trained me to keep one eye on the technology and one on the actual need, instead of just heads-down coding.

I have since completed my master’s degree and thesis on camera-based movement assessment for Parkinson’s disease. During the research, I worked with neurologists at Kaohsiung Medical University Hospital, and the results came close to their scoring. The work explored how an ordinary camera could support objective assessment, with home use as a research goal.

During my studies, I also completed industry-academia projects in smart manufacturing and image processing with companies, ITRI and National University of Kaohsiung. These collaborations have concluded and gave me experience in model development, system integration and on-site testing. I continue to value systems that work reliably in practice, beyond a polished demonstration.

我喜歡寫程式，因為能把想法做成實際有人使用、也能解決問題的工具。

高中就讀臺南高商資料處理科時，我開始接觸程式設計，經常用 Visual Basic 製作視窗小遊戲。看著自己的想法真的在電腦上執行，是當時最大的成就感。高中期間也參加文書處理競賽，獲得全國高級中等學校商業類學生技藝競賽金手獎。

進入高雄科技大學資訊管理系後，我開始用程式解決生活中的問題。大一時花了一天開發自動選課工具，幫自己選到想修的課，後來也持續提供同學使用。資管的學習背景，讓我在開發前會先釐清使用需求，再選擇適合的技術。

我已取得資訊管理碩士學位，並完成以攝影機分析巴金森氏症患者動作的碩士論文。研究期間與高雄醫學大學附設醫院神經內科醫師合作，研究結果接近醫師的評分。這項研究以居家評估為應用方向，目標是降低患者與家屬的操作門檻。

在學期間，我也完成了智慧製造與影像處理相關的產學合作計畫，合作對象包括業者、工研院及高雄大學團隊。這些合作現已結案，也讓我累積了模型開發、系統整合與現場測試的經驗。對我而言，系統能否在實際使用環境中穩定運作，始終是開發時的重要考量。
